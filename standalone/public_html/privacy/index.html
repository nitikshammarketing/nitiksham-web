PRIVACY & ENQUIRIES

Clear communication.
From the start.

The enquiry form prepares a message on your device. It does not store your details in a website database. WhatsApp or your email application opens only when you choose to continue; review the message before sending it.

WhatsApp and your email provider process messages under their own privacy terms. For questions about information you send Nitiksham, contact contact@nitiksham.com.

Studio account, uploads and software links

The private Studio uses your signed-in account identity to restrict management access to the website owner. Uploaded designs, newsletter files, titles, descriptions and software links are stored on the server so they remain available across devices.

Unpublished uploads and software access links stay within the owner workspace. Publishing a gallery item makes its file available to visitors who can access this website. Publishing a software listing displays its name and description; live and demo access links remain private.

Archiving removes an item from display but retains its stored file. For permanent removal requests, contact contact@nitiksham.com. External software and demo applications apply their own authentication and data policies.

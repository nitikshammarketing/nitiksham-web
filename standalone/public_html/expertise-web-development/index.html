02 — HOW IT WORKS

Through our five-step process

Every website follows the same five-step process — Discover, Plan, Build, Launch, Grow — so design, content and development stay in step from the first meeting to long after launch.

01

Discover

We study your business, your customers and your current website, and agree what success looks like.

02

Plan

Structure, content and design direction are agreed before building starts, so there are no surprises later.

03

Build

Design and development move together, with your content — written in-house or by our copywriting team — fitted to every page.

04

Launch

Testing across devices and browsers, then a careful launch, with on-page SEO in place from day one.

05

Grow

After launch, we review how visitors use the site and keep improving the pages that matter most.

03 — QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

01

What makes a good business website?

One that answers a visitor’s questions quickly, looks like the business it represents, and makes the next step obvious.

02

Do you build landing pages?

Yes. We build focused landing pages for campaigns, launches and lead generation, designed around a single offer and a single action.

03

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website we build is responsive, so it adapts to phones, tablets and desktops. Most visitors arrive on a phone, so we design for the small screen first.
